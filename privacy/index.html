Privacy Policy

Effective August 10, 2026

AddTogether is designed to help families coordinate daily life while keeping their information under their control. This policy explains how AddTogether handles information.

Information you add

AddTogether may contain family member names, roles, profile photos, chores, completion photos, points and rewards, grocery lists, pantry items, calendar events, meal plans, recipes, and recipe photos that you choose to add.

Where information is stored

App information is stored locally on your device. If you choose Premium family syncing, supported family information is also stored in Apple iCloud and may be shared with people you invite to your AddTogether family. Apple operates iCloud under its own terms and privacy policy. Photos currently remain on the device where they were added. AddTogether does not sell your personal information.

Subscriptions and purchases

Premium subscriptions are purchased and managed through Apple’s App Store. Apple processes payment and subscription information. AddTogether receives purchase status needed to unlock Premium features but does not receive your full payment-card details.

Device permissions

AddTogether asks only for permissions needed for features you choose to use. These may include Photos or Camera for profile, chore, inventory, event, and recipe images; Calendar for Apple Calendar import and export; iCloud for family syncing; and Notifications for reminders. You may change permissions in Apple Settings.

External services

Store buttons may open third-party retailer websites or apps. Those services have their own privacy practices. AddTogether does not control information you provide directly to them.

Children’s information

AddTogether is intended for use under parent or guardian supervision. Parents control the family information entered into the app and the people invited to a shared family. AddTogether does not operate an advertising or data-sale service directed to children.

Deleting information

You can edit or delete information using controls inside the app. Removing the app removes locally stored app data from that device, subject to device backups managed by Apple. Information previously synced through iCloud may remain in iCloud or on a shared participant’s device until it is deleted there or the shared family is removed.

Policy changes

This policy may be updated as AddTogether adds or changes services. Any updated policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy or support may be sent to support.addtogether@gmail.com.
