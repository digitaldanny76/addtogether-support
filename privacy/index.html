Privacy Policy

Effective August 9, 2026

AddTogether is designed to help families coordinate daily life while keeping their information under their control. This policy explains how the current version of AddTogether handles information.

Information you add

AddTogether may contain family member names, roles, profile photos, chores, completion photos, points and rewards, grocery lists, pantry items, calendar events, meal plans, recipes, and recipe photos that you choose to add.

Where information is stored

The current version stores app information locally on your device. AddTogether does not operate an account server and does not sell your personal information.

Device permissions

AddTogether asks only for permissions needed for features you choose to use. These may include Photos or Camera for profile, chore, inventory, event, and recipe images; Calendar for Apple Calendar import and export; and Notifications for reminders. You may change permissions in Apple Settings.

External services

Store buttons may open third-party retailer websites or apps. Those services have their own privacy practices. AddTogether does not control information you provide directly to them.

Children’s information

AddTogether is intended for use under parent or guardian supervision. Parents control the family information entered into the app. We do not knowingly collect children’s information on an AddTogether-operated server in this version.

Deleting information

You can edit or delete information using controls inside the app. Removing the app from a device also removes its locally stored app data, subject to any device backups managed by Apple.

Policy changes

This policy may be updated as AddTogether adds services such as family syncing or accounts. Any updated policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy or support may be sent to support.addtogether@gmail.com.
